A local writing notebook, inspired by Jason Fried.

Download the ZIP for your computer, **extract the whole folder**, then double-click **Start write_better**. Your browser opens automatically. No Node.js installation or account is needed for writing. Keep the launcher window open while using the app.

| Computer                                       | Download                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 and newer) | `write-better-darwin-arm64.zip` |
| Mac with an Intel processor                    | `write-better-darwin-x64.zip`   |
| Windows with Intel/AMD processor               | `write-better-win32-x64.zip`    |
| Linux with Intel/AMD processor                 | `write-better-linux-x64.zip`    |

These initial bundles are unsigned; your operating system may ask you to approve the downloaded launcher. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/Bayonle/write-better#install) for details. AI is optional and requires separate setup.

Drafts are saved locally, outside the app folder. Stop the old launcher before opening an updated download. Use **Export notebook backup** to keep an extra copy of your writing.

Source archives below are for developers; most people should choose one of the four bundles above.
