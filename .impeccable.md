# Design context

Confirmed with the user on 2026-10-03 during the critique workflow.

## Users and purpose

write_better. serves all three goals the user selected: a calm personal writing space, an approachable product for writers, and an efficient, keyboard-friendly Markdown tool. The core task is developing a draft while comparing alternate wording in its surrounding prose. Writers should be able to keep discarded ideas nearby, format Markdown, and optionally request AI assistance.

## Brand and aesthetic

The reference is the Jason Fried writing tool shared in the conversation. The current product uses an intimate, editorial, typewriter-inspired aesthetic with night and paper themes. Preserve its writing-first personality while improving clarity and discoverability as it becomes a fuller product.

## Design principles

- Keep the draft and its surrounding sentences readable when comparing alternatives.
- Reveal advanced tools progressively so casual writing stays calm and expert work stays efficient.
- Make the basic flow discoverable without requiring shortcut knowledge.
- Give explicit, understandable save and recovery feedback.
- Make formatting, alternatives, and recovery usable across keyboard and touch input.

The principles synthesize the user's confirmed audience goals and prior feature requests; they are proposed design guidance rather than additional user-selected priorities.
